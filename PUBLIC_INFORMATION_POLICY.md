# IKHOE — Public Information Policy

IKHOE Labs follows an **open architecture, closed advantage** approach.

The public surface communicates:

- what IKHOE products do;
- what has actually been demonstrated;
- aggregate progress and current status;
- public integration interfaces;
- evidence that can be independently inspected.

The public surface does not intentionally disclose:

- secrets or credentials;
- private infrastructure;
- customer data;
- private prompts or internal model information;
- proprietary algorithms, heuristics or parameters;
- internal routing, promotion, compensation, repair or reconfiguration mechanisms whose disclosure would materially enable reconstruction of competitive advantages.

## Status principle

`IMPLEMENTED → FUNCTIONING → CONNECTED → EXECUTED → EFFECT → OBSERVED → VERIFIED → PROVED`

Public claims use the strongest state supported by evidence, never a stronger one.

## Progress
